Two certificates for the projection constant

Appendix L of the First Edition reads the projection constant nine times, on three structural levels. These are the machine-checkable counterparts of that appendix: one executable, one kernel-checked.

They answer a narrow question, and it is worth stating the question before the answer. Given the structural inputs declared by the monograph, are the representations of the projection invariant algebraically coherent? Not whether QGT is true, not whether the invariant is the measured fine-structure constant. Those are different questions, and no amount of computation settles them.

1. B1 — symbolic-executable verifier pass

A Python harness built on exact symbolic algebra. It constructs the objects Appendix L describes — a bilinear form, a singular value decomposition, a transfer function, a dilatation residue, a quadratic form in Z[√5], a regular matrix representation — and checks the declared identities on them. Nine typed checks, twenty-eight lemmas.

Three of the nine directly produce the invariant, and they agree symbolically, not to a number of decimals:

I.4   Pincherle residue        30*sqrt(5) + 70
II.1  Fibonacci norm           30*sqrt(5) + 70
II.2  regular representation   30*sqrt(5) + 70
      reference 20*phi**4  ->  137.08203932499369089227...

The right name for this is exact cross-representation coherence. It is not independent numerical convergence: the three are representations of one algebraic element, and their agreement certifies that the representations cohere, not that nine separate experiments concurred.

Finite negative controls

Keeping everything else fixed and replacing the imported rank by its neighbours, the downstream arithmetic stops closing:

rankcrdetreadout
3(2,1)−19 + 4√5does not close

6(8,5)−61(8+5√5)²does not close

This is a sensitivity test over a finite declared set. It shows the arithmetic is sensitive to the rank input. It is not a proof that five is the only possible rank: that theorem is upstream and is not re-proved here.

2. Formal pilot — Lean 4 kernel-checked

The arithmetic core of the same appendix, re-proved inside a proof assistant. Here the certificate is not a boolean returned by a program: it is a proof term the Lean kernel has accepted.

lake build                    Build completed successfully
sorry 0    admit 0    unsafe 0    native_decide 0

QGT.pincherleReadout          does not depend on any axioms
QGT.fibonacciNormReadout      does not depend on any axioms
QGT.regularMatrixReadout      does not depend on any axioms
QGT.crossRepresentation       does not depend on any axioms
QGT.finiteRankSensitivity     does not depend on any axioms

Ten of the twelve exported theorems depend on no axioms at all. The other two use propext and Quot.sound, standard logical axioms of Lean. No custom axiom exists in the project, and none carries a QGT conclusion.

The carrier is the ring of integers of Q(√5) in the basis {1, φ}, built from pairs of integers. Multiplication is induced by φ² = φ + 1, so that identity is a theorem of the representation, not an axiom. The project has no external dependency: it builds from Lean core alone, so a referee reproduces it with the toolchain and nothing else.

What is imported, and what is proved

rolecontent
importedrank five; the coefficient vector is the Fibonacci pair
provedφ²=φ+1, (√5)²=5, Λ²=20φ⁴, cᵀGc=20φ⁴, eval(M²)=20φ⁴, their mutual equality, and the finite rank sensitivity

The imported inputs are a named structure whose fields appear in the signature of every theorem that uses them. B1 consumes the rank-five selection; it does not re-prove its uniqueness.

Scope read this before citing

Neither artefact establishes the physical truth of QGT, nor the empirical identity of the invariant with the measured fine-structure constant. No measured value of α, no CODATA figure and no fitted parameter enters either of them at any point. Rank five is an imported input in both, not a result. The negative controls are finite. Readings I.2, I.3, III.1 and III.3 are covered only by the Python harness, at its lower level of assurance; they need real or analytic structure and are deliberately left out of the formal pilot rather than approximated.

The authority is the monograph. These are derived from it, and each declares the frozen version it was built against. What the kernel guarantees is that the Lean statements are proved; that they are the statements the monograph makes is human work, and SOURCE_MAP.md inside the project exists so a referee can check that correspondence line by line.
