INTRO 2 / 6

How QGT gets to hydrogen

The atom is the end of a physical chain, not an initial assumption.

  • Finite access: the QGT CELL exposes only a physically accessible part of the parent dynamics.
  • Reciprocal gluing: the two counter-oriented access walls remove forbidden mixing and organize the accessible sector.
  • Matter: the Schur reduction of accessible and retained sectors selects a stable complex matter line.
  • Electromagnetism: the occupied stable doublet leaves one unbroken massless U(1) branch.
  • Binding: the static response of that massless branch gives the Coulomb potential.
  • Hydrogen: the two-body sector closes to the hydrogenic Schrödinger problem.

The three physical acts

Once the QGT atomic sector is available, the simulator asks three different questions.

  • PREPARE — Can the Coulomb sector support the expected bound ground state? A broad trial wavefunction is numerically projected toward 1s.
  • LIVE — What does an already formed quantum atom do in real time? Bound components interfere, phase evolves and probability current flows through the cloud.
  • LIGHT — Does the atom respond to an external electromagnetic probe? A near-resonant electric pulse polarizes the cloud and transfers population into the 2p channel.

INTRO 5 / 6

What the light pulse means physically

The light is not a decorative animation and it is not the old radial “breathing” drive.

An explicit electric field is coupled to the atomic dipole. When its frequency is near the 1s ↔ 2p transition, the initially symmetric cloud becomes polarized: its centre of charge moves, the dipole oscillates and population is transferred into the excited channel.

That is the crucial readout: the QGT-derived bound sector is not only stable — it can be prepared, driven and measured like an atom.

What is closed here — and what is not

This simulator marks a precise boundary in the QGT programme.

Closed here: a Schrödinger-grade atomic sector with Coulomb binding, bound-state dynamics and an explicit dipole response to light.

Not claimed here: a full simulation of spontaneous radiative recombination from the continuum, which would require the quantized emission sector, nor a complete empirical closure of QGT as a theory of all interactions.

What matters is that QGT now reaches a familiar, dynamical and interrogable physical object: hydrogen.
