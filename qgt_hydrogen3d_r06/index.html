What you will NOT see

Three modes, three physical meanings

The simulator uses the dimensionless Schrödinger-grade Hamiltonian H₀ = −½∇² − 1/ρ, with explicit dipole coupling −F(τ)z in LIGHT mode.

Important: PREPARE is a numerical preparation of the bound state. It is not presented as a physical radiative recombination. LIGHT, by contrast, genuinely contains the dipole term −F(τ)z.
